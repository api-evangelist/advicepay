In the world of security and compliance, time is the ultimate validator. Longevity doesn’t mean you’re immune to problems. It means you’ve had to operate through change: evolving expectations from enterprise buyers, shifting regulatory focus, new attack patterns, vendor-review checklists that get longer every year, and the day-to-day reality of running a platform people rely on.
